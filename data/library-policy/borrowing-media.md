# Borrowing Media

## Georgetown users

Undergraduate and graduate students, faculty, staff, postdoctorate/affiliated/visiting researchers, Library Associates, Medstar Staff, and Continuing Education Students may borrow an unlimited number of media items for **2 weeks** with **2 renewals**.

Media can be renewed through a HoyaSearch account, by phone at (202) 687-7607, or in person.

## Consortium and other borrowers

WRLC students, faculty, and staff may borrow an unlimited number of media items for **7 days**. Non-Georgetown affiliated faculty and college students, and library visitors, have library-use-only access. Corporate borrowing is not allowed.
