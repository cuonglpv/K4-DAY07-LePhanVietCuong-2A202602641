# Interlibrary and Consortium Loans

Books & Media

Check HoyaSearch. Georgetown books located on HoyaSearch as "Available," allow patrons to look for the item on site.

If the item is not available at Georgetown, but is available at a consortium library, select Consortium Request.

If the item is not available at Georgetown or through the WRLC, an Interlibrary Loan (ILL) may be available. After a request is placed, ILL staff attempt to find an available copy from another library and notify the requester if it is unavailable.

Items from Other Locations

HoyaSearch items designated "Available at Lauinger Library Off-Campus Storage" or "Consortium Libraries" generally take 1-3 business days to arrive.

HoyaSearch items designated "Interlibrary Loan Request" may be shipped from around the world or other parts of the country. Average delivery times for Interlibrary Loans are 7-14 business days.

Recalls & Holds

Hold Shelf Items

Notifications are sent for every item once the item has been placed on the hold shelf and is ready to be picked up.

Recall Notices

Items recalled to be placed on Course Reserves must be returned in seven days, and take priority over items available to an individual loan. Items that have been recalled for Course Reserves will be made available to students that need access to materials. Course Reserves are especially useful for heavy or expensive textbooks, but may apply to any items needed for a course.

Recall notices are automatically generated and e-mailed. You should regularly check your HoyaSearch Account on the Library's website to check for recalls on your items in case the recall notification e-mail ends up in a spam folder.

Materials are subject to recall at ANY TIME. If you plan to be out of town for an extended period of time, please return borrowed items or arrange with someone to receive notices and return recalled material for you. Main Campus libraries are open during intersessions, so recalled items may be due any time the libraries are open.

Recall Fines

Recall fines apply to all borrowers and are $5 per item per day. There is no maximum limit on recall fines.
