# Equipment Loans

## Requesting equipment

From an equipment page, select **Reserve this item**, open HoyaSearch, and log in to a library account to make a request.

Media Equipment Checkout / Reservations

You must have a valid Georgetown GoCard to borrow equipment. See the Library's Borrowing Policies page for more information.

Reservations must be made at least 1 day in advance. It will be on hold shelf for up to 24hrs of pickup date

The pickup location is the main Service Desk on the third floor of Lauinger Library.

Most equipment can be checked out for up to 72 hours (3 days).

Equipment must be returned at the front desk to a student, not left unattended

For a longer extension please see our Extended Loans Form

You may check out a maximum of 1 PA system, 2 cameras, and 20 items in total.

What you check out is a public device. You are responsible for clearing any data on the device and logging off of any personal accounts before returning it. The device will remain checked out to any patron who does not log out of their personal account when a device is returned.

Renewals

You may renew an item depending upon its availability.

To renew items please email equipment.fines@georgetown.edu , or call (202) 687-7607. This will only be done once virtually per patron. 

Fines

The late fine for equipment is $1 per hour.

Your account will be blocked if you have overdue equipment. Bring your equipment back on time! See more fine information on our Fines Overview page.

Borrowers will be assessed replacement charges for lost or broken items, and for equipment more than one week overdue. A $30 processing fee will be added to the replacement costs and any overdue fines. Charges will be added to the student's account through the Bursar's office.

Borrowing privileges may be revoked if a user accrues more than $30 in fines, shows a history of returning items late, or disregards our Library Use Policies.

Questions?

If you need assistance or have questions, please call (202) 687-7607, email accessservices@georgetown.edu or for equipment & fines please email equipment.fines@georgetown.edu
